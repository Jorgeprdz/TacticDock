# R1.4.1 — DeX-only notification popups

- Tactic Dock's custom notification overlay is created only AFTER the Dock view has attached to a **live external display**, and only when the overlay's WindowManager context matches that exact display.
- Notification callbacks are marshalled to the main thread.
- The popup is suppressed when the monitor is gone or the window does not belong to the Dock's current display; on restart all timers and animation callbacks are disposed and window teardown is isolated per view.
- The custom popup appears at the external desktop's upper-right corner, not at the bottom of the phone display.
- The user's phone notifications are native Android/SystemUI heads-up; no app-controlled replacement, global interruption setting, or suppression is introduced.
- No Samsung SystemUI modifications; native heads-up depends on Android channel/system settings and cannot be forced by an ordinary third-party app.
